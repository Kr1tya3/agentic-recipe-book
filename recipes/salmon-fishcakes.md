# Salmon Fishcakes

Soft potato and salmon cakes with a crisp oat coating, baked in the oven. A good way to get oily
fish into children who don't like the look of a fillet. Makes 8.

## Steps

1. Boil the potatoes in unsalted water for 15 minutes until tender. Add the peas for the last
   2 minutes. Drain well and mash roughly.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 200°C fan (220°C conventional). Bake the salmon on a lined tray
   for 12-15 minutes until it flakes. Leave the oven on.
3. Flake the salmon into the mash, checking for bones, and mix in the lemon zest.
4. Shape into 8 cakes. Dust each with flour, dip in egg, then press into the oat crumbs.
5. Brush a baking tray with half the oil, add the fishcakes, and drizzle the rest of the oil on
   top. Bake for 20 minutes, turning halfway, until golden.
6. Serve with steamed broccoli.

## For little ones

Check carefully for bones when flaking. Children like these with a little yogurt or ketchup to
dip.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Freeze the uncooked, coated cakes on a tray, then bag them. Bake from frozen for 30 minutes.

## Swaps

Use 2 x 200 g tins of salmon (drained, skin and large bones removed) instead of fresh fillets.
