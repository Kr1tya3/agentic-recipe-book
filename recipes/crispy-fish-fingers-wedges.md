# Homemade Fish Fingers with Sweet Potato Wedges

Crunchy oven-baked fish fingers with sweet potato wedges and peas: the classic, made with plain
white fish and a crisp oat coating. Uses freezer fish, so it can be cooked any day of the week.

## Steps

1. The night before, move the fish from the freezer to a covered plate in the fridge to defrost.
2. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Toss the sweet potato wedges with 1 tbsp of oil and half the
   paprika on a baking tray. Roast for 30 minutes, turning halfway.
3. Meanwhile, pat the fish dry with kitchen paper and cut it into finger-sized strips.
4. Blitz the oats into fine crumbs and mix with the lemon zest, the rest of the paprika and the
   remaining 2 tbsp of oil. Put the flour, the beaten egg and the oat crumbs in three shallow bowls.
5. Dip each strip in flour, then egg, then oat crumbs, pressing them on. Lay on a second lined
   baking tray.
6. Bake the fish for 15-18 minutes, adding it to the oven after the wedges have had 12 minutes,
   until golden and the fish flakes easily.
7. Cook the peas in boiling water for 3 minutes. Serve with the yogurt dip and a squeeze of lemon.

## For little ones

Break one fish finger open to check it is cooked through and to look for any bones. Cut wedges
into thin sticks for the youngest. Squash the peas slightly for under-2s.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Best eaten fresh; leftover fish fingers keep 1 day in the fridge and can be reheated in the oven
until piping hot. Don't refreeze fish that was frozen when bought.

## Swaps

Use 4 salmon fillets (`salmon-fillets`) for an oily-fish version. Breadcrumbs from 3 slices of
stale wholemeal bread can replace the oats. Use potatoes instead of sweet potatoes for classic
chips.
