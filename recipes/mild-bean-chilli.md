# Mild Bean and Sweetcorn Chilli

A warming, smoky but not spicy chilli made from tins and jars. Red lentils thicken it like mince
would, and a pinch of cinnamon brings out the sweetness of the tomatoes. Children can top their
own bowls with cheese and yogurt.

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over low heat. Stir in the garlic granules, paprika, cumin,
   cinnamon, oregano and tomato purée and cook for 1 minute.
2. Add the kidney beans, lentils, tomatoes and 300 ml of water. Bring to the boil, then simmer for
   25 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils are soft and the chilli is thick. Add a
   splash more water if it gets too dry.
3. Meanwhile, cook the rice according to the packet.
4. Stir in the sweetcorn for the last 5 minutes.
5. Serve with rice or warm tortillas, and bowls of grated cheese and yogurt to add at the table.
   Adults can add chilli sauce or flakes to their own plates.

## For little ones

Mash some of the beans with the back of a spoon in the pan, or squash the children's portion with
a fork: whole kidney beans have tough skins that small children can choke on.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Keeps 3 days in the fridge and freezes well for 3 months. Leftovers make a good quesadilla
filling (see [Cheese and Sweetcorn Quesadillas](cheese-sweetcorn-quesadillas.md)) or a
jacket potato topping.

## Swaps

Add a fresh chopped onion, carrot or red pepper at step 1 when you have them. Use chickpeas or a
mix of tinned beans instead of kidney beans. Vegan and gluten-free without the optional toppings
and tortillas.
