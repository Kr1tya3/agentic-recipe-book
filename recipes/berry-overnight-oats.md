# Berry Overnight Oats

Prepared the evening before; nothing to cook in the morning. The frozen berries thaw overnight
and turn the oats pink, which children tend to like.

## Steps

1. The evening before, mash the banana in a large bowl or container.
2. Stir in the oats, milk, yogurt and cinnamon.
3. Fold in the frozen berries. Cover and refrigerate overnight (at least 8 hours).
4. In the morning, stir and add a splash of milk if it is too thick. Serve cold, or warm the
   children's portions for 30-45 seconds in the microwave.

## For little ones

Squash larger berries (blueberries, blackberries) before serving to toddlers.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Keeps 3 days in the fridge, so a double batch covers several breakfasts.

## Swaps

Use a grated apple instead of the banana, or any frozen fruit instead of berries.
