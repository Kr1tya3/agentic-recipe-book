# Sweet Potato and Lentil Dahl

A gentle, slightly sweet curry. Mild curry powder gives flavour without heat.

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat and cook the onion for 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and curry powder and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the lentils, sweet potato, tomatoes, crumbled stock cube and 700 ml of water. Bring to the
   boil, then simmer for 25 minutes, stirring often so it doesn't stick, until the lentils have
   broken down and the sweet potato is soft. Add more water if it gets too thick.
4. Meanwhile cook the rice according to the packet.
5. Stir the spinach into the dahl until wilted. Serve with rice and a spoonful of yogurt.

## For little ones

Mash any larger sweet potato pieces for younger children. A spoon of yogurt cools it down.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Freezes well. Cook double the rice and keep the extra (cooled quickly, refrigerated) for
[chicken fried rice](chicken-fried-rice.md) the next day.

## Swaps

Vegan with coconut yogurt and a vegan stock cube. Use carrot or squash instead of sweet potato.
