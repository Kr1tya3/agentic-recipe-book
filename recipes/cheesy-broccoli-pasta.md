# Cheesy Broccoli Pasta

Macaroni cheese with green vegetables cooked in the same pot as the pasta.

## Steps

1. Cook the pasta in a large pan of unsalted boiling water according to the packet. Add the
   broccoli for the last 4 minutes and the peas for the last 2.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the flour and cook
   for 1 minute.
3. Gradually whisk in the milk and keep whisking until the sauce thickens and bubbles, about
   5 minutes.
4. Take off the heat and stir in most of the cheese until melted.
5. Drain the pasta and vegetables, stir through the sauce, and top with the rest of the cheese.

## For little ones

Cook the broccoli a minute longer if your toddler prefers it very soft. Mashing a few florets
into the sauce makes them harder to pick out.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Best fresh. Leftovers reheat with a splash of milk, or bake in a dish with extra cheese on top.

## Swaps

Use cauliflower instead of, or with, the broccoli.
