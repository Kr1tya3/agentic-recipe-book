# Slow Cooker Minestrone Soup

A thick Italian vegetable, bean and pasta soup that is almost a stew. Start it in the morning and it
is ready for lunch or dinner.

## Steps

1. Put the onion, garlic, carrot, courgette, potato, chopped tomatoes, kidney beans, crumbled stock
   cube and oregano in the Instant Pot. Add 1 litre of water and stir.
2. Put on the lid with the valve set to **Venting** (or use a glass slow-cooker lid). Cook on
   **Slow Cook, More (high)** for 4 hours, or **Slow Cook, Normal** for 7 hours.
3. Stir in the pasta and switch to (or stay on) **Slow Cook, More**. Cook for 25-30 minutes more,
   until the pasta is soft. Add a little boiling water if it gets too thick.
4. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Serve with grated cheese and bread.

## For little ones

Serve it thick, with more vegetables and pasta than broth, so it can be eaten with a spoon or
fingers. Squash the beans for children under 2. Let it cool well first.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Freezes for up to 3 months; the pasta goes softer, which young children often prefer. The pasta
keeps soaking up liquid, so add water when reheating until piping hot.

## Swaps

Chickpeas instead of kidney beans. Use only tinned beans: dried kidney beans must be boiled hard
before slow cooking, as slow cooking doesn't make them safe. Swap the courgette for frozen peas
(add with the pasta).
