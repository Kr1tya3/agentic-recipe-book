# Instant Pot Red Lentil Bolognese

A meat-free bolognese that tastes like the classic. Red lentils melt into the sauce and the
grated vegetables disappear, so it suits children who pick out "bits".

## Steps

1. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté**. Heat the oil and cook the onion, carrot and courgette for
   5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and oregano for 30 seconds. Press **Cancel**.
2. Dissolve the stock cube in 450 ml of hot water. Pour it in and scrape the bottom of the pot
   clean, then stir in the lentils.
3. Spoon the tomato purée on top and pour the passata over it. **Do not stir**, so the thick
   tomato stays off the bottom of the pot and doesn't trigger the "burn" warning.
4. Lock the lid, set the valve to **Sealing** and cook on **Pressure Cook, High** for 8 minutes.
5. Meanwhile cook the pasta in a large saucepan according to the packet.
6. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then turn the valve to **Venting**. Stir
   well. If it is too thin, simmer on **Sauté** for a few minutes, stirring.
7. Serve over the pasta with grated cheese.

## For little ones

Mix the sauce into short pasta for toddlers so it is easy to spoon. For very fussy eaters, blend
the sauce smooth.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Doubles easily (keep the pressure time the same; don't fill the pot past the max line, as lentils
foam). Freezes for up to 3 months. Reheat until piping hot, adding a splash of water.

## Swaps

Use it in place of the beef sauce in a lasagne or on a baked potato. Red pepper instead of
courgette. Half lentils, half beef mince also works (brown the mince on Sauté first).
