# Hidden Veg Bolognese

A mild meat sauce with three vegetables and some lentils blended into the tomato base, so
picky eaters see only a smooth red sauce.

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion, carrot and courgette for
   8 minutes until soft.
2. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Tip the softened vegetables into a jug.
3. Turn the heat up and brown the mince in the same pan for 5 minutes, breaking it up.
4. Blend the vegetables with the chopped tomatoes until smooth (or skip this if your family
   doesn't mind seeing them).
5. Add the tomato mixture, lentils, crumbled stock cube, oregano and 200 ml of water to the
   mince. Simmer, partly covered, for 25 minutes, stirring now and then. Add a splash of water
   if it gets too thick.
6. Meanwhile cook the pasta in unsalted water according to the packet. Serve the sauce on the
   pasta with a little cheese.

## For little ones

Chop spaghetti into short lengths, or use small pasta shapes, for toddlers.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Doubling the sauce costs about 5 minutes more. Freeze portions for 3 months. Leftover sauce also
works on a jacket potato or as a filling for wraps.

## Swaps

Use turkey mince or replace the meat with 150 g more red lentils (then vegan, if you check the
stock cube and skip the cheese).
