# Creamy Salmon and Pea Pasta

A 20-minute dinner with oily fish, in a mild creamy sauce.

## Steps

1. Cook the pasta in unsalted boiling water according to the packet. Add the peas for the last
   2 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat and cook the salmon for 4 minutes per
   side until it flakes easily. Flake into large pieces, removing any bones.
3. Scoop out a mugful of pasta water, then drain the pasta and peas.
4. Return the pasta to the pan over low heat. Stir in the cream cheese, milk and spinach until the
   spinach wilts and the sauce is creamy. Add a splash of pasta water if it is too thick.
5. Gently fold in the salmon, lemon zest and a squeeze of juice.

## For little ones

Check the salmon for bones once more as you flake it.

## Swaps

Use a 200 g tin of salmon, or leftover cooked salmon, and skip step 2.
