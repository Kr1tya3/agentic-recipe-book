# Mild Chicken Fajita Wraps

Everything goes on the table in bowls and everyone fills their own wrap, which helps reluctant
eaters try things.

## Steps

1. Toss the chicken with the paprika and cumin.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Cook the chicken for 5-6 minutes until
   browned and cooked through. Move to a plate.
3. Cook the pepper and onion in the same pan for 5 minutes until soft. Add the sweetcorn and the
   chicken back in for 1 minute.
4. Warm the tortillas (10 seconds each in the microwave or in a dry pan).
5. Put the filling, cheese, yogurt and lemon wedges on the table and let everyone build their own.

## For little ones

Small children may prefer an open "plate" version: chicken, pepper strips and a torn tortilla
side by side, rather than a rolled wrap.

## Swaps

Adults can add chilli flakes or hot sauce to their own wraps.
