# Veggie Egg Muffins

Mini baked omelettes. Makes 12; good warm for breakfast or cold in a lunchbox.

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan (200°C conventional). Grease a 12-hole muffin tin with the oil.
2. Divide the pepper, spinach, sweetcorn and half the cheese between the holes.
3. Whisk the eggs and milk together, then pour into the holes until each is three-quarters full.
   Sprinkle with the rest of the cheese.
4. Bake for 18-20 minutes until puffed and set in the middle.
5. Leave for 5 minutes, then loosen the edges with a knife and lift out.

## For little ones

Make sure the middle is fully set (no runny egg) for young children.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Fridge for 3 days or freeze for a month. Reheat for 30-40 seconds in the microwave, or eat cold.

## Swaps

Any finely chopped leftover vegetables work: broccoli, peas, courgette.
