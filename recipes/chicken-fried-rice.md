# Chicken and Veg Fried Rice

Quicker than a takeaway. Cold, day-old rice fries best, so this is a good way to use rice cooked
for another meal.

## Steps

1. If you are not using leftover rice: cook the rice, spread it on a tray to cool quickly, and
   refrigerate within 1 hour (see the safety note below).
2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat. Pour in the eggs, scramble until just set, then
   tip onto a plate.
3. Add the rest of the oil and stir-fry the chicken for 5 minutes until cooked through.
4. Add the carrot and garlic for 2 minutes, then the peas and sweetcorn for 2 minutes.
5. Add the rice and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until piping hot. Stir in the egg and soy sauce.

## For little ones

Soy sauce is salty: 2 tablespoons of reduced-salt sauce across the whole pan is plenty. Adults
can add more at the table.

## Food safety

Cooked rice must be cooled quickly and kept in the fridge for no more than 1 day before frying.
Reheat only once, until steaming hot throughout.

## Swaps

Use leftover chicken from the traybake, or prawns. Tamari makes it gluten-free.
