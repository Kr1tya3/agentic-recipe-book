# Instant Pot Mac and Cheese with Peas and Sweetcorn

The pasta cooks in just enough water to be absorbed, so there's no draining, then cheese and milk
are stirred in to make a creamy sauce. Peas and sweetcorn add colour and sweetness.

## Steps

1. Put the pasta and garlic granules in the Instant Pot and pour in 650 ml of cold water. Press
   the pasta down so it is just covered.
2. Lock the lid, set the valve to **Sealing** and cook on **Pressure Cook, High** for 4 minutes
   (half the packet time, minus 1 minute, if your pasta's packet time differs from 10 minutes).
3. Release the pressure straight away by turning the valve to **Venting**. Cover the valve with a
   tea towel if it spits.
4. Stir in the milk, frozen peas and sweetcorn. Set to **Sauté** and stir for 2 minutes until the
   vegetables are hot.
5. Press **Cancel**, then stir in the cheddar and cream cheese until melted and creamy. If it is
   too thick, add a splash more milk. Sprinkle with paprika to serve.

## For little ones

Let it cool for a few minutes: cheese sauce holds its heat. Squash sweetcorn kernels for children
under 2.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Best eaten fresh. Leftovers keep 2 days in the fridge; reheat with a splash of milk, stirring,
until piping hot.

## Swaps

Use broccoli florets instead of the sweetcorn: steam them on top of the pasta (they go soft under
pressure, which suits younger children). Stir in a drained tin of tuna to add protein.
