# Jacket Potatoes with Cheesy Beans and Broccoli

Fluffy baked potatoes with beans and melting cheese, and broccoli "trees" on the side. Almost all
of the cooking time is hands-off, which makes it a good choice for a busy afternoon.

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Prick the potatoes all over with a fork, rub with the oil
   and bake directly on the oven shelf for 50-60 minutes (small ones 40-45 minutes) until the
   skins are crisp and a knife slides in easily.
2. In the last 10 minutes, warm the beans gently in a saucepan. Steam or boil the broccoli for
   4-5 minutes until tender.
3. Split the potatoes, fluff the insides with a fork and add a little butter if you like.
4. Spoon over the beans and top with the cheddar so it melts.

## For little ones

Scoop the potato out of the skin and mash it with the beans and cheese for younger children; the
skins can be tough to chew. Let it cool well, as baked potatoes stay very hot inside.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Baked potatoes keep 2 days in the fridge; reheat in the oven or microwave until piping hot.
To save time, microwave the potatoes for 8-10 minutes first, then crisp them in the oven for
20 minutes.

## Swaps

Use sweet potatoes (about 40 minutes). Top with leftover [bean chilli](mild-bean-chilli.md)
instead of baked beans, or tuna mixed with sweetcorn and yogurt. Baked beans contain some salt;
for a no-salt version, warm a drained tin of kidney beans with 250 g of passata and a pinch of
paprika.
