# Chicken and Sweet Potato Traybake

Everything roasts on one tray: little washing up and about 10 minutes of hands-on work.

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan (220°C conventional).
2. Toss the chicken, sweet potato, pepper and onion with the oil and paprika on a large roasting
   tray. Spread out in one layer (use two trays if crowded, or it steams instead of roasting).
3. Roast for 20 minutes.
4. Add the broccoli, turn everything over, and roast for 15 minutes more, until the chicken is
   cooked through and the sweet potato is soft.
5. Squeeze over the lemon before serving.

## For little ones

Cut chicken into thin strips or small pieces. Roasted sweet potato cubes are a good first finger
food.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Leftover chicken and vegetables go well in wraps or the fried rice the next day.

## Swaps

Use ordinary potatoes or carrots instead of sweet potato; courgette instead of broccoli.
