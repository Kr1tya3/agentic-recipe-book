# Slow Cooker Hidden Veg Pasta Sauce

Five vegetables and red lentils cook down slowly, then get blended into a smooth, sweet tomato
sauce. No chopping skill needed, because everything is blended at the end.

## Steps

1. Put all the vegetables, the lentils, chopped tomatoes, passata, tomato purée, oregano, crumbled
   stock cube and olive oil in the Instant Pot. Add 150 ml of water and stir.
2. Put on the lid with the valve set to **Venting** (or use a glass slow-cooker lid). Cook on
   **Slow Cook, More (high)** for 4 hours, or **Slow Cook, Normal** for 7 hours, until the
   vegetables are very soft.
3. Blend in the pot with a stick blender until smooth (switch the pot off first and take care, it
   is hot).
4. Cook the pasta in a large saucepan according to the packet, drain and stir through the sauce.
   Serve with grated cheese.

## For little ones

Smooth sauce on short pasta is ideal for toddlers. It also works as a dip for toast fingers or a
pizza topping on a tortilla.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Make a double batch (the pot holds it easily) and freeze the sauce in portions for up to 3 months.
Use it in place of passata in the [meatballs](mini-meatballs-tomato-sauce.md) or as a quick
sauce on busy nights.

## Swaps

Use sweet potato instead of the carrot, or add a handful of baby spinach before blending. Leave out
the lentils for a lighter sauce.
