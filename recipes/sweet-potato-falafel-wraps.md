# Baked Sweet Potato Falafel Wraps

Soft, golden falafel bites made with sweet potato, baked rather than fried, served with a lemony
yogurt dip and a build-your-own spread of wraps and crunchy vegetables. The sweet potato makes
them sweeter and softer than shop falafel, and children love dipping and rolling their own.

## Steps

1. Microwave the sweet potato in a covered bowl with a splash of water for 6-8 minutes until very
   soft (or steam it for 12 minutes). Drain well and let it steam dry for a few minutes.
2. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan) and brush a baking tray with 1 tbsp of the oil.
3. Blitz the chickpeas, sweet potato, garlic, cumin, paprika, lemon zest and flour in a blender
   until mostly smooth with a little texture. The mix should hold its shape; add a spoonful more
   flour if it is too wet.
4. With wet hands, roll into about 24 walnut-sized balls, then flatten each slightly into a
   little patty. Lay them on the tray and brush with the remaining oil.
5. Bake for 25-30 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and firm on the outside.
6. Meanwhile, mix the yogurt with a squeeze of lemon juice for the dip, and put the grated carrot,
   pepper strips and spinach in small bowls.
7. Warm the tortillas and let everyone fill their own wraps, or serve the falafel as finger food
   with the dip and vegetables on the side.

## For little ones

Flattened patties are easier and safer to eat than round balls; break them in half for under-3s.
Serve the wrap cut into pinwheels, or skip the wrap and offer falafel, dip and vegetable sticks
on a plate. Cut the pepper into thin strips rather than chunks.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Cooked falafel keep 3 days in the fridge and are good cold in a lunchbox. Freeze cooked falafel
for up to 3 months and reheat from frozen at 180°C for 12-15 minutes. The uncooked mix keeps
1 day in the fridge.

## Swaps

Serve with rice or pitta instead of wraps. A carrot can
replace part of the sweet potato. For a cupboard version, replace the fresh garlic with 0.25 tsp
garlic granules.
