# Banana Oat Pancakes

Small, soft pancakes sweetened only by banana. Makes about 16 pancakes, 8 cm across.

## Steps

1. Blend the oats in the blender until they look like flour.
2. Add the bananas, eggs, milk, baking powder and cinnamon. Blend until smooth, then let the
   batter rest for 5 minutes so the oats thicken it.
3. Heat a frying pan over medium heat and wipe it with a little butter.
4. Spoon in about 2 tablespoons of batter per pancake. Cook for 2 minutes until bubbles appear,
   flip, and cook 1-2 minutes more. Repeat with the rest of the batter.
5. Serve with yogurt and berries.

## For little ones

- Cut into strips for toddlers to pick up. Squash or halve blueberries and other round berries.
- The browner the bananas, the sweeter the pancakes.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Cool completely, then freeze in a bag with baking paper between layers. Reheat from frozen in a
toaster or for 30 seconds in the microwave.

## Swaps

- Any milk works, including oat milk (then the recipe is milk-free if you skip butter and yogurt).
