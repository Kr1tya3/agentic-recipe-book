# Mild Chickpea and Coconut Curry

A creamy, gently spiced curry made entirely from the cupboard. The coconut milk keeps it mild and
slightly sweet, and a few red lentils melt into the sauce to thicken it.

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over low heat. Stir in the garlic granules, curry powder,
   cumin and tomato purée and cook for 1 minute, stirring so the spices don't catch.
2. Add the chickpeas, lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk and 200 ml of water. Bring to the boil, then
   simmer for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils have broken down and the sauce
   is thick.
3. Meanwhile, cook the rice according to the packet.
4. Optional: stir in the frozen peas for the last 3 minutes.
5. Serve over the rice, with a spoonful of yogurt if you like.

## For little ones

Whole chickpeas are a choking risk for babies and the youngest toddlers: squash them with a fork
or blend the children's portion briefly. A spoonful of yogurt cools it down.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Keeps 3 days in the fridge and freezes well in portions for 3 months (freeze without the rice).
Cook double rice and use the rest for [fried rice](chicken-fried-rice.md) the next day.

## Swaps

Use fresh onion and garlic when you have them. Add a chopped sweet potato or a handful of baby
spinach for extra vegetables. A tin of kidney beans can replace one tin of chickpeas.
