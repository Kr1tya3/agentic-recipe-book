# Tuna and Sweetcorn Tomato Pasta

A store-cupboard pasta that is on the table in 20 minutes. The smooth sauce has no lumps of
onion to pick out, and the sweetcorn gives little bursts of sweetness children look for.

## Steps

1. Cook the pasta in unsalted boiling water according to the packet.
2. Meanwhile, warm the oil in a saucepan over low heat. Stir in the garlic granules, tomato purée
   and oregano and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the passata and simmer gently for 8-10 minutes until slightly thickened.
4. Stir in the tuna, broken into flakes, and the sweetcorn. Heat through for 2 minutes.
5. Scoop out a mugful of pasta water, drain the pasta and stir it into the sauce. Loosen with a
   splash of pasta water if needed.
6. Optional: sprinkle with grated cheddar to serve.

## For little ones

Flake the tuna finely so there are no big chunks. Let the pasta cool for a few minutes before
serving.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Keeps in the fridge for 2 days and is good cold in a lunchbox. Not recommended for freezing.

## Swaps

Use a tin of salmon instead of tuna for oily fish. Add a handful of frozen peas to the pasta water
for the last 2 minutes, or a chopped tin of tomatoes instead of passata (blend it for picky
eaters). A clove of fresh garlic can replace the granules.
