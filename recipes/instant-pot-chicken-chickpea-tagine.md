# Instant Pot Mild Chicken and Chickpea Tagine

Tender chicken and chickpeas in a lightly sweet tomato sauce warmed with cinnamon and cumin. No
heat at all, so it suits the whole family.

## Steps

1. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté**. Heat the oil and cook the onion for 3 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika and cinnamon and stir for 30 seconds, then add the chicken and
   stir for 2 minutes. Press **Cancel**.
3. Dissolve the stock cube in 150 ml of hot water, pour it in and scrape the bottom of the pot
   clean. Add the carrots and chickpeas.
4. Pour the chopped tomatoes on top and **do not stir**: tomatoes on the bottom of the pot can
   trigger the "burn" warning.
5. Lock the lid, set the valve to **Sealing** and cook on **Pressure Cook, High** for 8 minutes.
6. Meanwhile cook the rice in a saucepan according to the packet.
7. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then turn the valve to **Venting**. Stir.
   If the sauce is thin, simmer on **Sauté** for 3-5 minutes.
8. Serve over the rice with a spoonful of yogurt and a squeeze of lemon.

## For little ones

Shred the chicken with two forks and squash the chickpeas for children under 3, as whole
chickpeas can be a choking risk for very young children. The yogurt cools and softens it.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Freezes well for up to 3 months without the rice. Defrost overnight in the fridge and reheat until
piping hot.

## Swaps

Use sweet potato chunks instead of carrot. Vegetarian: leave out the chicken, add a second tin of
chickpeas and 300 g of sweet potato, and cook for 5 minutes.
