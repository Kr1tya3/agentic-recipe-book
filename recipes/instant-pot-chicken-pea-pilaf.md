# Instant Pot Chicken and Pea Pilaf

Chicken, rice and vegetables cooked together in one pot, gently spiced and fluffy. The rice soaks
up the stock, so it is full of flavour without any added salt.

## Steps

1. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté**. Heat the oil and cook the onion for 3 minutes.
2. Add the chicken, garlic, carrot, cumin and paprika and stir for 2 minutes until the chicken is
   no longer pink on the outside. Press **Cancel**.
3. Dissolve the stock cube in 330 ml of hot water. Pour a little into the pot and scrape the
   bottom clean (this stops the "burn" warning), then add the rest of the stock and the rice.
   Push the rice under the liquid but don't stir it further.
4. Lock the lid, set the valve to **Sealing** and cook on **Pressure Cook, High** for 5 minutes.
5. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then turn the valve to **Venting** to
   release the rest.
6. Scatter the frozen peas on top, put the lid back on (no pressure) for 3 minutes so they warm
   through, then fluff everything up with a fork.
7. Serve with a spoonful of yogurt and a squeeze of lemon.

## For little ones

Check the chicken pieces are small enough for your child, and cut any bigger ones up. Rice and peas
are easy for toddlers to pick up with a spoon or fingers; let it cool a little first.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Rice must be cooled quickly (spread it out on a plate) and refrigerated within an hour. Eat within
1 day and reheat until piping hot. Do not reheat more than once.

## Swaps

Use frozen sweetcorn instead of peas, or diced red pepper instead of carrot. For a vegetarian
version, swap the chicken for a drained tin of chickpeas.
