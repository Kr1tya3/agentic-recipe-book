# Instant Pot Carrot and Sweet Potato Soup

A bright orange, naturally sweet soup that children recognise and enjoy. Red lentils thicken it
and add protein; coconut milk makes it silky.

## Steps

1. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté**. Heat the oil and cook the onion for 3 minutes, then add the
   garlic and cumin for 30 seconds. Press **Cancel**.
2. Add the carrot, sweet potato, lentils, crumbled stock cube and 800 ml of water. Scrape the
   bottom of the pot to loosen anything stuck.
3. Lock the lid, set the valve to **Sealing** and cook on **Pressure Cook, High** for 8 minutes.
4. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then turn the valve to **Venting**.
5. Stir in the coconut milk and blend with a stick blender until smooth. Add a little water if it
   is too thick.
6. Serve with a swirl of yogurt and bread or toast for dipping.

## For little ones

Serve it lukewarm in a cup or with toast fingers for dipping. It also works as a pasta sauce for
fussy eaters.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Freezes well in portions for up to 3 months. Reheat until piping hot. Freeze the rest of the
coconut milk tin in an ice-cube tray, or use it the same week for the
[chickpea curry](chickpea-coconut-curry.md).

## Swaps

Use all carrot, or potato instead of sweet potato. Milk instead of coconut milk works too (stir it
in after blending, don't boil it).
