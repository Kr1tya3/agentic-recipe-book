# Slow Cooker Beef and Vegetable Stew

A classic stew where the beef turns meltingly soft and the potatoes cook in the gravy, so it is a
whole meal in one pot. Rich in iron, which small children need.

## Steps

1. Toss the beef in the flour. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté**, heat the oil and brown the beef in
   two batches, 2-3 minutes each. Add the onion and garlic with the last batch. Press **Cancel**.
   (You can skip browning on a busy morning; the stew is just a little paler.)
2. Dissolve the stock cube and tomato purée in 400 ml of hot water. Pour it in and scrape the
   bottom of the pot clean.
3. Add the carrots, potatoes and oregano and stir so everything is mostly covered.
4. Put on the lid with the valve set to **Venting** (or use a glass slow-cooker lid). Cook on
   **Slow Cook, More (high)** for 5 hours, or **Slow Cook, Normal** for 8 hours, until the beef is
   tender.
5. Stir in the frozen peas, cover and leave for 5 minutes until hot. Serve with bread for the
   gravy.

## For little ones

Cut the beef into small pieces or shred it with a fork, and mash the potato and carrot into the
gravy for younger children.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Tastes even better the next day. Freezes for up to 3 months; defrost overnight in the fridge and
reheat until piping hot.

## Swaps

Sweet potato instead of some of the potato. Short on time: pressure cook it instead, on **Pressure
Cook, High** for 30 minutes with a 15-minute natural release (use 300 ml of water). Gluten-free:
leave out the flour and thicken by mashing a few potato chunks into the gravy.
