# Mini Meatballs in Tomato Sauce

Small, soft meatballs that hide a whole courgette, baked rather than fried. Makes about 24.

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan (220°C conventional) and line a baking tray.
2. In a bowl, mix the mince, oats, egg, courgette and half the garlic with your hands.
   Roll into walnut-sized balls and place on the tray.
3. Bake for 15 minutes until browned and cooked through.
4. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large saucepan and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the rest of
   the garlic for 1 minute, then the tomatoes and oregano. Simmer for 10 minutes. Blend if your
   children prefer a smooth sauce.
5. Cook the rice according to the packet.
6. Add the meatballs to the sauce and simmer for 5 minutes. Serve over the rice.

## For little ones

Halve or quarter the meatballs for toddlers. Check one is cooked through with no pink inside.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Freeze the meatballs in their sauce for 3 months. Leftovers make a good lunch in a wrap.

## Swaps

Serve with wholemeal pasta instead of rice. Turkey or pork mince also work.
