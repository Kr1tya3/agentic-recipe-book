# Cheese and Sweetcorn Quesadillas

Toasted folded wraps filled with melted cheese and vegetables. Fast enough for a weekday lunch.

## Steps

1. Mix the cheese, sweetcorn, pepper, spinach and paprika in a bowl.
2. Spread the filling over half of each tortilla and fold over.
3. Cook in a dry frying pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes per side, pressing down with a
   spatula, until golden and the cheese has melted. Do two at a time if they fit.
4. Cut each into 3-4 wedges. Serve with yogurt for dipping and carrot sticks.

## For little ones

- Let the wedges cool for a couple of minutes: melted cheese stays very hot.
- For under-3s, steam the carrot sticks until soft or grate the carrot instead.

## Swaps

Add mashed tinned beans or leftover shredded chicken for more protein.
