# Slow Cooker Chicken and Sweetcorn Chowder

A thick, creamy soup full of soft potato, sweetcorn and shredded chicken. Mild and sweet, it is a
filling one-bowl meal on a cold day.

## Steps

1. Put the onion, garlic, potato and carrot in the Instant Pot and lay the chicken on top. Add the
   crumbled stock cube, paprika and 700 ml of water.
2. Put on the lid with the valve set to **Venting** (or use a glass slow-cooker lid). Cook on
   **Slow Cook, More (high)** for 4 hours, or **Slow Cook, Normal** for 6-7 hours.
3. Lift out the chicken and shred it with two forks.
4. Lightly mash some of the potato in the pot to thicken the soup. Stir in the sweetcorn, milk,
   cream cheese and shredded chicken.
5. Set to **Sauté** and heat for 5 minutes, stirring, until hot and the sweetcorn is cooked. Don't
   let it boil hard or the milk may split. Serve with bread.

## For little ones

Shred the chicken finely and let it cool. Squash sweetcorn kernels for children under 2, or blend a
portion smooth.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Keeps 2 days in the fridge; reheat gently until piping hot. Dairy-based soups with potato don't
freeze well (the texture goes grainy).

## Swaps

Use frozen peas instead of half the sweetcorn. Vegetarian: leave out the chicken and add a drained
tin of chickpeas or extra potato, and stir in 40 g of grated cheddar at the end.
