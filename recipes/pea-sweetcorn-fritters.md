# Pea and Sweetcorn Fritters

Golden vegetable pancakes made almost entirely from freezer and cupboard ingredients. Makes
about 12.

## Steps

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, eggs and milk into a thick batter.
2. Roughly crush the peas with a fork (so they don't roll off a toddler's fork), then stir them
   into the batter with the sweetcorn and cheese.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Drop in heaped tablespoons of
   batter and flatten slightly.
4. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Repeat with the rest of the
   batter and oil.
5. Serve with yogurt for dipping and a few raw vegetable sticks for the adults.

## For little ones

Crushing the peas also removes the choking risk of whole peas for under-2s.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Freeze cooled fritters with baking paper between them. Reheat in a hot oven for 8-10 minutes.

## Swaps

Add a handful of chopped spinach or grated courgette (squeeze it dry first).
