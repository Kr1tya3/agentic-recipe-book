# Slow Cooker Pulled Chicken Wraps

Chicken thighs cook slowly in a smoky, mild tomato sauce until they fall apart. Everyone fills their
own wrap, which children love.

## Steps

1. Put the onion and garlic in the Instant Pot. Mix the paprika, cumin, tomato purée and passata
   in a jug with 50 ml of water and pour half over the onion.
2. Lay the chicken thighs on top and pour over the rest of the sauce.
3. Put on the lid with the valve set to **Venting** (or use a glass slow-cooker lid). Cook on
   **Slow Cook, More (high)** for 4 hours, or **Slow Cook, Normal** for 6-7 hours.
4. Shred the chicken in the pot with two forks and stir it into the sauce. If the sauce is thin,
   simmer on **Sauté** for 5 minutes with the lid off.
5. Warm the tortillas. Put the chicken, sweetcorn, yogurt, cheese and spinach on the table and let
   everyone fill their own wraps.

## For little ones

Serve the chicken and sweetcorn on a plate with a torn-up tortilla for younger children, or roll a
small wrap and cut it into pinwheels. Squash sweetcorn for children under 2.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Doubles easily. The shredded chicken freezes in its sauce for up to 3 months; reheat until piping
hot. Leftovers are good in [quesadillas](cheese-sweetcorn-quesadillas.md) or with rice.

## Swaps

Serve on rice or in a baked potato instead of wraps. Red pepper strips instead of sweetcorn.
