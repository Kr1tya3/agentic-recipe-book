# Tomato and Red Lentil Soup

A smooth, mild, naturally sweet soup. The lentils make it filling without any meat. Blended
smooth, the vegetables disappear.

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion and carrot for 5 minutes
   until softened.
2. Add the garlic and cumin and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the lentils, tomatoes, stock cube and 1 litre of water. Bring to the boil, then simmer
   for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils are soft.
4. Blend until smooth. Add a little more water if it is too thick.
5. Optional: grill the toast with cheddar on top and cut into soldiers for dipping.

## For little ones

Cool the children's bowls with a spoonful of cold water or a splash of milk.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Freezes well in portions for 3 months. Thickens in the fridge; loosen with water when reheating.

## Swaps

Swap one carrot for a chunk of sweet potato or squash. Vegan only without the cheese toast.
